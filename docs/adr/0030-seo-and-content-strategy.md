# ADR-0030: SEO & content strategy

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-05-04
- **Slice:** 1

## Context

The site needs to be discoverable for "<city> poker club", "private poker room <city>", and similar searches. Good SEO is mostly: fast pages, proper metadata, structured data, useful content. Marketing routes are public; member surfaces (`/admin`, `/cashier`, `/dashboard`) are not and must be excluded from indexing.

## Decision

Marketing pages are **server-rendered** via React Server Components so every public route ships fully-rendered HTML — no client-side gating of SEO content. Technical SEO, content surfaces, and a performance budget are all owned in-repo; off-site local SEO (GBP, Apple, Yelp) is an owner task tracked in the spec's open questions.

### Technical SEO

- **Metadata.** `app/(marketing)/layout.tsx` sets defaults; each page overrides `title` and `description`.
- **Open Graph + Twitter cards.** Hero image (chip logo) by default, with a per-page OG image generated by `app/og/route.tsx`.
- **Sitemap.** `app/sitemap.ts` enumerates all marketing routes plus scheduled tournaments.
- **Robots.** `app/robots.ts` allows `/` and `/(marketing)/*`; disallows `/admin`, `/cashier`, `/dashboard`, and `/api`.
- **Structured data (JSON-LD):** `LocalBusiness` on `/contact` (address, hours, phone), `Event` on `/games/[slug]` per tournament, `Organization` on `/` (logo, social, sameAs).

### Content

- **NAP consistency.** Hours, address, and phone surface prominently on `/contact` and in the footer.
- **Photography.** Venue, room layout, and signage photos for image search and Google's local panel.
- **FAQ.** A `/faq` page lands in Slice 1 and expands in Slice 4 to capture long-tail queries.

### Performance budget

Lighthouse performance ≥90 enforced in CI, with field-style targets of LCP <2.5s, CLS <0.1, INP <200ms. Images go through `next/image` (AVIF + WebP); the hero photo lazy-loads below the fold. Fonts use `next/font` with `display: swap` to avoid FOIT.

### Local SEO (out of code scope)

Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect, and Yelp/TripAdvisor presence are owner tasks documented in `docs/spec.md` open questions; not blocking for code launch.

> **Open question:** Whether to ship a blog (event recaps, strategy posts) post-launch — deferred.
>
> **Open question:** Whether to invest in paid search (Google Ads) at launch — deferred to owner.

## Consequences

**Positive:**

- Every marketing page is crawlable on first byte; no JavaScript-rendered content gates ranking signals.
- The `app/sitemap.ts` + `app/robots.ts` pair makes index/no-index intent explicit and reviewable in PRs, reducing the risk of accidentally indexing `/cashier` or `/admin` after a refactor.
- Structured data on `/contact` and `/games/[slug]` qualifies the site for rich-result eligibility (local panel, event carousel) at zero ongoing vendor cost.
- A CI-enforced Lighthouse budget keeps perf regressions from shipping; this also lifts the a11y floor (ADR-0026 shares the ≥90 Lighthouse threshold).
- No SEO-specific vendor dependency: everything lives inside Next.js, so there is no recurring SaaS cost or API quota to manage.

**Negative:**

- Per-tournament JSON-LD couples marketing rendering to the tournament data model (ADR-0012). A schema change there requires a marketing-side update, with the risk of stale or invalid `Event` markup in the wild.
- Auto-generated OG images via `app/og/route.tsx` shift cost onto edge invocations and depend on Vercel's runtime; an outage there silently degrades social previews.
- The Lighthouse ≥90 budget can collide with rich photography (the venue photos called out under "Content") and with consent-banner scripts (ADR-0024); LCP work will need ongoing attention.
- Crawler analytics are partly dark: the consent default is "deny" (ADR-0024), so Slice-1 SEO-funnel data in PostHog (ADR-0028) only captures consenting visitors. We rely on Vercel Analytics and Search Console for unconsented traffic shape.
- NAP consistency is now a release-time concern: changing the venue phone or address means editing the footer, `/contact` JSON-LD, sitemap-driven pages, and the off-site listings in lockstep, or local SEO score degrades.
- Owner-task local SEO (GBP, Apple, Yelp) sits outside the codebase and outside CI; if not done, the on-site work above under-delivers.

## Alternatives considered

- **Client-rendered marketing site (CSR / SPA).** Faster iteration on interactive landing experiences, and trivially deployable as a static bundle. Rejected: Googlebot does render JS but with delay and quirks, and Bing/DuckDuckGo are weaker at it; for a local-search-first business, server-rendered HTML is the safer ranking floor. Next.js RSC gives us SSR without giving up the interactive ergonomics.
- **Third-party SEO/CMS platform (Webflow, Framer, Wix).** Faster non-engineer content edits and built-in sitemap/OG handling. Rejected: it splits the surface area (member app on Next.js, marketing on a separate platform), forces duplicate brand/design systems, adds a recurring SaaS line item, and makes structured-data control coarser than what `LocalBusiness`/`Event` JSON-LD allows in-repo.
- **Defer structured data and OG images to a later slice.** Lower Slice-1 scope. Rejected: `LocalBusiness` + `Event` schema is the highest-leverage SEO win for a local poker venue, and OG images materially affect click-through from social shares of tournaments. The cost is a few small files (`app/og/route.tsx`, JSON-LD components) and is cheaper now than retrofitting after launch.
